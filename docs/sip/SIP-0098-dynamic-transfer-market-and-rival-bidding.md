# SIP-0098 Dynamic Transfer Market and Rival Bidding

Status: Proposed

Introduce visible rival bids, market pressure, countdowns, AI counter-offers and urgency mechanics.

Goals:
- Make transfer market the primary daily engagement feature.
- Notify users when outbid.
- Show number of interested clubs.
- Create flash opportunities lasting 2-12 hours.
- Increase login frequency.

Acceptance:
- Transfer offers can trigger counter-bids.
- Market news highlights bidding wars.
- Users receive notifications for competitive offers.
